Support ticket counts come from the Q3 support report.

Background

Users can only turn all notifications on or off today. In Q3, 38% of support tickets about notifications asked to mute a single channel. See the Q3 support report for the full breakdown.

The email digest setting was removed in v4 and is out of scope.

Goals

  • Mute a channel in two clicks or fewer.
  • Keep the current defaults for existing users.
    • Email and push stay on.
    • SMS stays off.
  • Store settings under user.notifications.

Open Questions

Team accounts

Can an admin lock settings for all members? Deferred to the next release.

Security alerts must never be muted, whatever the settings say.
Push tokens expire after 90 days. Settings must survive a token refresh.

Rollout

  2. Enable it for internal usersShould beta testers be included?No, internal users only. for one week.
  3. Roll out to everyone if the ticket count drops.
